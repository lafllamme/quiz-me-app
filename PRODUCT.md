# Jungle Quiz

Jungle Quiz is a local-first team quiz for two teams playing in the same room.
The host sets up the teams, chooses categories, reads questions aloud, and
resolves answers while the interface keeps the pace visible.

## Audience and scene

Friends at a birthday, dinner, or living-room game night. One laptop or large
screen is shared by the group; interactions should be legible from a short
distance and easy to operate with a keyboard.

## Primary job

Start a game quickly, make the next decision obvious, and keep score and time
readable without turning the quiz into a dashboard.

## Product truth

- Two teams alternate turns.
- Each round begins with a category choice.
- Questions have four answer options, a timer, reveal, steal, and scoring.
- Current sample content is synthetic and replaceable.
- The existing Jungle / Quiz visual language is an established starting point.

## Success criteria

The host can understand what to do within seconds, players can scan the active
state from across a room, and each screen has enough personality to feel like a
designed game rather than a generic form.
